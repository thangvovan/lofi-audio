# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

android

## Users
People looking to relax, sleep, study, or focus, especially in low-light or nighttime environments, who require a continuous, soothing auditory background.

## Product Purpose
To provide a seamless, uninterrupted lofi radio streaming experience on Android devices, offering quick access to curated live audio channels while promoting sleep, relaxation, and deep focus.

## Positioning
A distraction-free, retro-themed ambient soundboard for nighttime relaxation and deep focus.

## Brand Personality
Retro, pixel-art, lo-fi nostalgic (classic 80s/90s vibe, cassette tape textures), cozy, minimal, and deeply immersive.

## Anti-references
Bright light themes and high-contrast colors that disrupt night relaxation.

## Design Principles
- **Immersive Nostalgia**: Integrate warm retro visual cues (e.g., pixel-art accents, cassette player motifs) to evoke comfort and nostalgic tranquility.
- **Restful Presentation**: Rely on soft, deep dark hues and dimmed accent lights to avoid visual stimulation, ensuring the UI remains comfortable in completely dark environments.
- **Unobtrusive Utility**: Controls must be minimal, fluid, and predictable. The interface should step out of the way once playback begins, fading gently into the background.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Enhanced contrast for dark surfaces, targeting a comfortable mid-contrast range for low-light legibility (at least 4.5:1 text contrast without being blindingly bright).
- Respect system reduced-motion settings, ensuring all visualizer or background animations have static or soft crossfade alternatives.
